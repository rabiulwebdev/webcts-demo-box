Our Heritage

Three generations of golden trust

What began in 1998 as a single workshop with two goldsmiths is today one of the region's most loved jewellery houses. Every Mandal piece passes through 27 hands — from design sketch to final polish — and leaves our atelier only when it deserves to carry your family's name.

We melt only BIS-hallmarked gold, set only certified stones, and price with fully transparent making charges. No hidden margins. No compromise. Ever.
